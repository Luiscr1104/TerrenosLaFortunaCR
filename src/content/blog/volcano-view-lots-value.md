# The Real Value of a Volcano View 🌋

Everyone wants that perfect shot of Arenal Volcano from their terrace. It’s the dream, right? But as an investor or a future retiree, you need to look beyond just the pretty picture. Not all volcano-view lots are created equal.

Here is what **actually** adds value to a property in La Fortuna.

## 1. The "Forever" View Guarantee 🔭

The most critical question: **Can someone build in front of you?**

> "A view is only valuable if it's yours forever. Don't pay a premium for a view that can be blocked by a two-story hotel next year."

When we evaluate land, we check the topography and zoning of neighboring lots. A lot on a ridge or with a steep drop-off in front is gold because your view is physically protected.

## 2. Infrastructure & Access 🛣️

You found a lot with a killer view, but do you need a 4x4 and a machete to get there?

*   **Paved Access**: Adds immediate value and rental appeal.
*   **Water & Electricity**: In Costa Rica, having legal water availability (*disponibilidad de agua*) is non-negotiable for building permits.
*   **Internet**: Fiber optic availability is a must for digital nomads and modern living.

## 3. Topography: Usable vs. Unusable Land ⛰️

A 5,000 m² lot might look big on paper, but if 80% of it is a 45-degree cliff, you’re paying for air.

**We look for:**
*   **Flat building pads**: Ready for construction.
*   **Gentle slopes**: Good for drainage and tiered landscaping.
*   **Stability**: Soil studies are crucial in volcanic regions.

## 4. The "Vibe" Factor ✨

Does the lot feel private? Can you hear the birds, or the highway?

*   **Privacy**: Natural barriers like trees or creeks.
*   **Sound**: Distance from main roads.
*   **Nature**: Mature trees and wildlife corridors add immense aesthetic and ecological value.

## Conclusion 🏡

Don't just buy a view; buy a **future**. A well-chosen lot with a protected view, good infrastructure, and usable land will always appreciate faster than a difficult lot with a fleeting glimpse of the volcano.
