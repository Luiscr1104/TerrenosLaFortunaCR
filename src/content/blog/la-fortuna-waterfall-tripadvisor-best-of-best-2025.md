# La Fortuna Waterfall Wins TripAdvisor "Best of the Best" Award for 2025! 🌍✨

We have incredible news for our community and everyone who loves the natural beauty of Costa Rica!

**The La Fortuna Waterfall has once again secured the international "Best of the Best" Travellers' Choice 2025 award from TripAdvisor.**

This prestigious accolade distinguishes destinations, hotels, and restaurants that rank within the **top 1% to 10% of the best-rated attractions by travelers globally.**

---

## What Makes This Award So Special? 🎖️

TripAdvisor analyzes millions of opinions collected between January 1st and December 31st to define the winners each year. This means the recognition comes directly from **you**—the visitors who share your real stories and experiences.

For the **second consecutive year**, the waterfall—managed by the Association for Integral Development of La Fortuna (ADIFORT)—has achieved this level of excellence.

> "From the moment tourists enter, they encounter a service of excellence designed to give them an experience they will remember and share," stated **José Eduardo Jiménez Vásquez**, Commercial Manager of ADIFORT.

---

## A Global Magnet for Nature Lovers 🗺️🌿

During 2024, the site welcomed nearly **1,000 visitors daily** from countries including the United States, Canada, England, Spain, Brazil, Mexico, Colombia, India, and France.

The waterfall's breathtaking 70-meter (230-foot) drop, located just four kilometers from the town center, remains a cornerstone of Costa Rican tourism.

### Visitor Perspectives:
*   *"It is a beautiful place that transmits the essence of nature; you must come and experience it for yourselves,"* shared **Ella Mae Butlig**, a tourist from Canada.
*   *"The recognition is well-deserved; it was so beautiful; we even saw two waterfalls at the same time,"* added **Brianna Karn** from the United States.

---

## More Than Just a Tourist Spot: A Community Pillar 🤝🏠

What many visitors don't realize is that their visit directly impacts the local community. ADIFORT has confirmed that the resources generated are reinvested into social, educational, cultural, and security programs in La Fortuna.

**Key community projects funded by the waterfall include:**
*   Beautification of the Central Park.
*   Community security cameras.
*   Student scholarships and school supplies.
*   Support for sports and cultural groups.
*   Assistance for people in street conditions.

"ADIFORT is focused on improving the quality of life for our neighbors and financing community programs thanks to every ticket we sell," Jiménez added.

---

## Planning Your Visit? 🎒💧

If you haven't experienced this world-class attraction yet, here is what you can expect:

*   **The Descent**: A challenge of 530 steps rewarded by pristine natural pools.
*   **Biodiversity**: Explore orchid trails, a butterfly garden, a frog pond, and even a "hotel" for stingless Mariola bees.
*   **Facilities**: The site offers a restaurant, souvenir shop, changing rooms, and safe rest areas with drinking water.
*   **Rates**: $10 for nationals and $20 for foreigners (with special rates for different conditions).
